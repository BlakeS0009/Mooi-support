Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Mooi ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. In short: Mooi is designed to keep your information on your device, and we don't sell or share your data.

Information the app uses

  • Skin profile. The skin type and skin concerns you choose, used to match scan results to your skin.
  • Scans. The ingredient lists you scan and the results, saved in your scan history so you can look back at them.
  • Settings. Your appearance and accessibility preferences.

You don't need an account to use Mooi, and we don't ask for your name, email address or location in the app.

Camera

Mooi asks for camera access so it can read ingredient lists. The camera is used only while you are scanning. Images are processed on your device to read the text and are not stored or uploaded.

Where your information is stored

Your skin profile, scan history and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Ingredient summaries are generated on your device.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Mooi does not contain advertising, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone.

Contacting us

If you contact us through the contact form or by email, we receive the name, email address and message you send. We use this only to reply to you. Messages sent through the contact form are delivered by a third-party form service, FormSubmit, which passes them on to our email.

Deleting your information

Because your information is stored on your device, you're in control of it. Deleting the app removes all of your Mooi data from your device. To have us delete an email you've sent us, just ask.

Children

Mooi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Not medical advice

Mooi provides general information about cosmetic ingredients. It does not diagnose or treat any skin condition.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
