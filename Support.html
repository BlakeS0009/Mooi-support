Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a product?

Tap the scan button in the middle of the bottom bar and point it at the product's ingredient list. Mooi will break down each ingredient and give you a short summary.

How do I get results matched to my skin?

Open My skincare (the smiley face icon) and choose your skin type and concerns. From then on, every scan highlights ingredients that suit you and flags the ones that might not.

Where can I find my past scans?

Tap the History icon on the right of the bottom bar. Every scan is saved there so you can look back and compare.

Can I make the app easier to read?

Yes. In Settings you'll find colour-vision modes, increased contrast, bold text, dyslexia-friendly text, reduced animations, and light and dark themes.

Is Mooi medical advice?

No. Mooi explains what ingredients are and what they're commonly used for. It doesn't diagnose or treat skin conditions. If you're worried about your skin, please speak with a dermatologist or your GP.

A product or ingredient looks wrong. What should I do?

Please email us with the product name and a photo of the ingredient list, and we'll take a look.
